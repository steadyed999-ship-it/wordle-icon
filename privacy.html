Our Daily Score – Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Our Daily Score is a free app for keeping score of a daily word puzzle with family and friends. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: it keeps only what it needs to show your group's scoreboard, and nothing is sold, tracked, or used for advertising.

Information the app stores

When you use Our Daily Score, the following is saved so your group can see the scoreboard:

  • Group details: the group name and its join code.
  • Player details: the name each player enters and the 4-digit PIN they choose.
  • Scores: the date, number of guesses, and the result text and grid a player shares or types in.

This information is stored in a private Google Sheet run by the app's developer through Google Apps Script. Google provides this service and handles the data under Google's Privacy Policy.

You don't need an email address or account to use the app. Please use a first name or nickname, and choose a PIN you don't use for anything else.

Information kept only on your device

  • The group you last used and the last player selected, so you don't have to pick them each time.
  • If you turn on "Remember me on this device", your player and PIN are saved on that device so you're logged in automatically. Tap "Switch player" to remove it.
  • If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your device. No reminder information leaves your device.

Who can see your information

Your name and scores are visible to the other members of your group, meaning anyone who has your group's code. Share your code only with people you want in your group. The developer can also see the stored information in order to run and fix the app.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone else.
  • No analytics, location, contacts, photos, or camera access.
  • The app only receives a puzzle result when you choose to share it to Our Daily Score.

Children

Our Daily Score is meant for general family use and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has entered information, contact us and it will be removed.

Deleting your information

You can delete your own scores in the app. To remove a player or an entire group, email ourdailyscore.app@gmail.com with the group code and player name, and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email ourdailyscore.app@gmail.com.

Our Daily Score is an independent app and is not affiliated with any puzzle publisher.
